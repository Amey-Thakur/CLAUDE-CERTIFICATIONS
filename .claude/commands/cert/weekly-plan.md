Produce one week of study for the certification in `$ARGUMENTS`.

## Gather what is missing

Ask for the certification, which week of preparation this is, and the hours available, unless a plan or diagnostic earlier in this session already established them. Use what is already known rather than asking again.

## Build

Read the certification's `README.md`, `notes.md`, and `guide/study-strategy.md`, plus `guide/courses.md` for the material that teaches each objective.

Lay out the week as dated sessions, each with:

- A duration that fits the stated hours, in blocks a person will actually sit through rather than idealised marathons
- One specific objective or small group of objectives from the blueprint, named exactly as the guide names them
- The specific material for it: the course lesson, documentation page, or article, linked from the repository's pages rather than described vaguely
- A concrete output for the session, so completion is verifiable: notes written, a schema designed, an agent loop running, a domain drilled to a stated score

Include across the week:

- At least one hands-on session that advances the build task from the plan
- One `/cert:drill` on the weakest domain
- A closing `/cert:mock` or `/cert:score-check` to measure the week

Front-load the heaviest blueprint domains early in the week when energy is highest, and put review at the end. If the hours are too few for the objectives targeted, cut objectives and say which were cut, rather than compressing everything into sessions that cannot be completed.
