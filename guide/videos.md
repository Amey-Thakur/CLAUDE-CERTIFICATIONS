# Official videos

Every video on this page is published by Anthropic on its own [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@anthropic-ai). Nothing here is third-party courseware. They are arranged by the exam they serve, in the order worth watching.

On the website each video plays inline. On GitHub the thumbnails link out to YouTube.

> [!TIP]
> Video is a supplement, not a substitute. The [22 official courses](courses.md) are the curriculum and the [exam guides](official-sources.md) define what is tested. Use these when a topic has not landed from reading, and when you want to hear how Anthropic's own engineers describe a decision.

## AI Fluency, the complete course

Anthropic publishes **AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations** as a full video course, and it is also [one of the 22 Academy courses](courses.md) with its own certificate. It underpins the Associate exam: the 4D framework, capabilities and limitations, and the prompting and judgment habits the scenarios assume.

The [complete playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLf2m23nhTg1NjL3-jL3s0qZCYzO07ZQPv) runs about 70 minutes. The lessons that carry the most exam weight:

### Lesson 2B: The 4D framework

Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence, the spine of the whole course and of the Associate blueprint.

[![Lesson 2B: The 4D Framework, from the Anthropic AI Fluency course](https://img.youtube.com/vi/W4Ua6XFfX9w/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4Ua6XFfX9w)

### Lesson 3B: Capabilities and limitations

What Claude can and cannot do, and how to tell the difference. This is the material behind the **AI Capabilities and Limitations** course and the questions that ask you to verify rather than trust.

[![Lesson 3B: Capabilities and limitations, from the Anthropic AI Fluency course](https://img.youtube.com/vi/W5cga7xipRI/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W5cga7xipRI)

### Lesson 7: Effective prompting techniques

The deep dive on prompting, and the longest lesson in the course.

[![Lesson 7: Effective prompting techniques, from the Anthropic AI Fluency course](https://img.youtube.com/vi/2YCaBqP8muw/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2YCaBqP8muw)
