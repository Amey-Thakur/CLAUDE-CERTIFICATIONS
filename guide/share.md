**A short post, for LinkedIn.**

```text
I worked through the Anthropic Claude certification curriculum and found the material scattered:
blueprints in one PDF, policies in another, courses behind a sign-in, and the practical details
written down nowhere.

So I collected all of it in one open repository:

- Every official exam guide and policy, mirrored with sources and dates
- The blueprint for each exam with real domain weights, so you study what is actually tested
- A one-page cheat sheet per exam for the day before
- 320 original practice questions, three mock exams per certification, and a shuffled timed engine
- Registration, proctoring setup, and the exam-day checklist

Free, no sign-up, and no exam content: everything is built from published material.

https://amey-thakur.github.io/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS/
```

## One thing to leave out

Never share exam questions, answer options, or scenario wording from a real sitting, and please do not invite others to. Every candidate signs a non-disclosure agreement, and a credential is only worth holding if the people holding it earned it. Everything in this repository is built from published material for exactly that reason.
