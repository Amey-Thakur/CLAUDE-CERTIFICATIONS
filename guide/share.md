## Copy worth using

**One line, for a message or a comment.**

```text
Free, open-source study guide for the Anthropic Claude certifications: every official exam guide,
the blueprints with real domain weights, one-page cheat sheets, and a practice engine.
https://amey-thakur.github.io/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS/
```

**A short post, for LinkedIn.**

```text
I worked through the Anthropic Claude certification curriculum and found the material scattered:
blueprints in one PDF, policies in another, courses behind a sign-in, and the practical details
written down nowhere.

So I collected all of it in one open repository:

- Every official exam guide and policy, mirrored with sources and dates
- The blueprint for each exam with real domain weights, so you study what is actually tested
- A one-page cheat sheet per exam for the day before
- 100 original practice questions with a shuffled, timed engine
- Registration, proctoring setup, and the exam-day checklist

Free, no sign-up, and no exam content: everything is built from published material.

https://amey-thakur.github.io/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS/
```

**For a study group or team channel.**

```text
If anyone is preparing for a Claude certification, this covers all four exams end to end:
blueprints, cheat sheets, practice questions, and the registration and proctoring details.
There is a printable booklet too if you prefer one file.
https://github.com/Amey-Thakur/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS
```

## What to attach

Posts with an image travel further than posts without one. Any of these work, and each carries the repository link so it leads back:

- The [roadmap](../.github/assets/roadmap.png), for a general audience
- The [cheat sheet](../associate-foundations/cheat-sheet.md) for the exam you are discussing
- The [exam-day checklist](../.github/assets/card-exam-day.png), which candidates tend to save
- The [six published scenarios](../.github/assets/card-architect-scenarios.png), for an Architect Foundations audience

## One thing to leave out

Never share exam questions, answer options, or scenario wording from a real sitting, and please do not invite others to. Every candidate signs a non-disclosure agreement, and a credential is only worth holding if the people holding it earned it. Everything in this repository is built from published material for exactly that reason.
