# Program changes

A dated record of everything Anthropic has changed about the Claude
certification program, and what each change means for a candidate now.

Certification programs change quietly. Fees move, platforms migrate, validity
periods are rewritten, and the pages describing them are edited in place with
no note that anything happened. Somebody who read the rules three months ago
and books on the strength of that memory is the one who gets caught.

Every entry below is dated, says what changed, and says who it still affects.

---

## 2026

### August: the academy moved to a new home

`anthropic.com/learn` now redirects permanently to
[academy.claude.com](https://academy.claude.com/), and the public academy is
branded **Claude Academy**. Course URLs changed shape with the move: a course
that lived at `anthropic.skilljar.com/claude-101` is now at
`academy.claude.com/courses/claude-101`, and several slugs were renamed rather
than simply moved.

**Who this affects.** Anyone holding bookmarks or following links from older
guides. The previous Skilljar pages still answer, so nothing is broken, but the
maintained home is the new one.

Completion badges already verified on `academy.claude.com` before the move, and
still do.

### August: the catalog grew to twenty-four courses

Two courses were added: **Deploying Claude Enterprise with Confidence**, on the
five organization-wide decisions behind a rollout, and **AI Fluency for pK-12
Train the Trainer**, built with the American Federation of Teachers.

**Who this affects.** Nobody sitting an exam. No course is required for any
certification, and the guides say so explicitly. It matters only if you are
working through the catalog.

### August 31: the Global Premier discount ends

Through August 31, 2026, Global Premier partners paid nothing. From September 1
the standard 50% partner discount applies.

**Who this affects.** Global Premier partners who were planning to sit an exam
in the autumn. Booking before the deadline was worth the full fee.

### July: exam guides published at version 1.0

All four exam guides carry version 1.0, effective July 2026. These are the
authoritative scope documents: the domains, the weights, the item counts, the
sample questions, and the scoring rules.

**Who this affects.** Everyone. If anything on this site disagrees with a guide,
the guide is right.

### June 30: delivery moved to Pearson VUE, badging to Credly

The largest change in the program's history, and the one with the longest tail.

| What changed | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Exam delivery moved to Pearson VUE | Booking, rescheduling, ID checks and the proctoring experience all run through Pearson now |
| Badging moved to Credly | Credentials issue as Credly badges rather than on the previous platform |
| Architect Foundations fee | Raised from 99 to 125 USD |
| Pre-migration failed attempts | Failed Architect Foundations attempts from before the migration were cleared, so those candidates could retake with no waiting period |
| Credential validity | Extended from 6 months to 12, applied automatically to credentials earned before the migration, dated from when they were originally earned |
| Registrations in flight | Moved to Pearson in waves |
| The official practice exam | Retired, and not replaced |

**Who this affects.** Anyone who registered or certified before June 30, 2026.
Two cases still cause trouble. If you paid before the migration and never
received a Pearson link, contact
[certifications-support@anthropic.com](mailto:certifications-support@anthropic.com)
rather than registering again, which charges you twice. And if a pre-migration
credential never appeared in Credly, the badge email may have gone to an
address you no longer read.

The retired practice exam is the change candidates notice most. There is no
official practice exam any more. The sample questions inside each exam guide
are the only official items that exist.

### June 25: the exam policy document was revised

The current [Anthropic Certification Exam
Policy](official-sources.md) carries a revision date of June 25, 2026. It
governs conduct, identification, prohibited items, invalidation and appeals.

**Who this affects.** Everyone sitting an exam. It is short, and it is the
document people wish they had read only after something goes wrong.

## How this page is kept

Program changes are found by re-downloading the mirrored official documents and
comparing them to the copies here, which happens on a schedule and before every
release. When a document changes, the change is recorded here with its date.

This page carries no rumors and no roadmap. A change appears once Anthropic has
published it, and not before.

If you know of a change that is not recorded here, please
[open a discussion](https://github.com/Amey-Thakur/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS/discussions)
with a link to the official source.
