# Claude Certifications

Study guides, official exam documents, and preparation resources for the Anthropic Claude certification program: Claude Certified Associate, Developer, and Architect. Every exam guide, policy document, blueprint, course path, and registration step, collected and organized in one place.

This is a community resource, maintained by [Amey Thakur](https://github.com/Amey-Thakur) after working through the program's curriculum, so that the next candidate can spend their time studying instead of searching. It is not an official Anthropic repository. The official program lives on [Anthropic Partner Academy](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/), and exams are delivered by [Pearson VUE](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/anthropic.html).

## The certifications

| Certification | Code | Questions | Fee (USD) | Guide | Details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claude Certified Associate – Foundations | CCAO-F | 60 | 99 | [PDF](pdfs/exam-guides/claude-certified-associate-foundations-exam-guide.pdf) | [Study page](docs/associate-foundations.md) |
| Claude Certified Developer – Foundations | CCDV-F | 53 | 125 | [PDF](pdfs/exam-guides/claude-certified-developer-foundations-exam-guide.pdf) | [Study page](docs/developer-foundations.md) |
| Claude Certified Architect – Foundations | CCAR-F | 60 | 125 | [PDF](pdfs/exam-guides/claude-certified-architect-foundations-exam-guide.pdf) | [Study page](docs/architect-foundations.md) |
| Claude Certified Architect – Professional | CCAR-P | 63 | 175 | [PDF](pdfs/exam-guides/claude-certified-architect-professional-exam-guide.pdf) | [Study page](docs/architect-professional.md) |

Common to all four exams: 120 minutes, multiple-choice and multiple-response, closed book, proctored by Pearson VUE online or at a test center, passing score 720 on a 100 to 1,000 scale, credential valid 12 months, free renewal before expiry, digital badge via Credly. Fees are list prices; Select, Preferred, and Global Premier partners receive 50% off, and Global Premier partners 100% off through August 31, 2026. Exams are available to Claude Partner Network organizations and require a recognized company email.

- You build with the Claude API, Claude Code, or MCP: [Developer – Foundations](docs/developer-foundations.md)
- You design Claude solutions end to end: [Architect – Foundations](docs/architect-foundations.md), then [Architect – Professional](docs/architect-professional.md)
- You are new to the program: [Learning paths and courses](docs/learning-paths.md)
- You have chosen an exam and want a plan: [Study strategy](docs/study-strategy.md)
- You are ready to book: [Registration and scheduling](docs/exam-registration.md)
